# Cubism learning map

Use the relevant [modeling](cubism-modeling-practice.md), [eyes/mouth](cubism-eyes-mouth.md) or [animation/export](cubism-animation-export.md) guide first. Open an official source when a feature/version gap needs it; this index is not a preload checklist.

Historical research on 2026-09-07 recorded 71 manual bodies, 25 tutorial bodies (10 substantive and 15 video-led), and 24 opened videos. Eighteen exported transcripts were read; six videos lacked transcripts and had only sampled frames. Five transcript-read videos also had frame samples. These are prior research claims, not fresh checks or evidence that every frame was watched. Exact original timestamps and coverage wording are retained in the pre-edit local backup manifest. Do not infer current Editor/API support or this avatar's acceptance from them.

Record new research as body/transcript/frames/index, with source date and unresolved scope. Verify captions' IDs/numbers/units against current manuals/UI. Old Cubism 3 demonstrations teach concepts, not current export compatibility; an alpha announcement is not an installed feature.
