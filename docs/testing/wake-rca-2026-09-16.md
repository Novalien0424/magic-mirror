## Confirmed implementation gaps

1. [Capture](../../src/main/wake/capture.ts) discards the native error and
   forwards only a generic failure. Native device-open/stream errors and the
   local malformed-buffer path therefore cannot be distinguished in telemetry.
2. [Worker acquisition](../../src/main/wake/worker.ts) acknowledges capture
   opening without waiting for the first audio block. In the pinned decibri
   implementation, stream startup is separate from asynchronous device open.
3. [Supervisor](../../src/main/wake/supervisor.ts) publishes `listening` on that
   acknowledgement. Its input snapshot marks audio `stalled` after three
   seconds without blocks, but this does not change the listening status,
   initiate recovery or emit a stalled transition event.
4. Explicit microphone failure restarts the worker once. That restart happened
   here, but the subsequent listening event cannot prove audio was restored.

Consequently, a successful reopen can appear healthy while audio processing is
stalled. The operator's live snapshot below confirms that condition in this run.
It establishes no block reports reached Main, rather than proving exactly which
native capture or detector operation stopped progressing.

## Recommended repair boundary

Preserve a safe, allowlisted microphone failure code; record capture-health
transitions and the resolved device selection without audio or transcript
content; distinguish opening from audio flowing; detect and recover missing
blocks with bounded release-before-reacquire behavior. Silence alone must not
trigger restart. Validate acquisition, stall recovery and microphone ownership
with focused tests, then verify physical speech on this Windows host. Threshold
tuning remains a separate evidence-dependent step.
